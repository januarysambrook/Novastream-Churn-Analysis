# Novastream-Churn-Analysis
Power BI customer churn analysis demonstrating data cleaning, modelling, DAX and business insight.

## Project Overview

NovaStream is a fictional subscription business experiencing customer churn.
The aim of this project was to identify the factors most strongly associated
with churn and recommend actions the business could take to improve retention.

The project was completed in Power BI as part of PL-300 training using a
synthetic dataset.

## Key Findings

- Overall churn rate was 23.9%.
- Monthly customers had substantially higher churn than annual customers.
- Customers without auto-renew enabled were considerably more likely to churn.
- Login inactivity was the strongest behavioural indicator of churn.
- Lower usage was associated with higher churn.
- Age and region showed relatively little relationship with churn.
